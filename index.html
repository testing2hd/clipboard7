    Download button for your sidebar

    Add the app called Download video to your sidebar or Edge panel. While a video is on screen, tap it: the app presses Share and Copy link and downloads the video. It first asks which folder to save to (set your folders in More settings → Files). In Chrome, Firefox, Samsung Internet, Brave and other browsers it reads the page address and downloads that page's video.
